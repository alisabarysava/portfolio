Experience & Approach

(02)

I have experience working with complex B2B and enterprise systems. I manage the entire product design process, from problem identification to handing over mockups to development.

I focus on building scalable systems, not individual screens. I excel at simplifying complex data architectures, adapting interfaces for mobile devices, and integrating AI solutions. I view constraints as catalysts for better design, not as obstacles.

I work closely with project managers and developers in cross-functional teams. I believe that the best products are created when design solves real business problems, based on data and a deep understanding of the user.
